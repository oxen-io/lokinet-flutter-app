Lokinet is a free and open source onion routing network. This app provides a VPN interface that routes your device's traffic through Lokinet on Android.

Features:
* Start and stop Lokinet with a single tap
* Optional exit node for reaching the regular internet (clearnet) through Lokinet
* Configurable upstream DNS
